# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — first public release

- **Modes:** Explore (smallest experiment that answers a design question), Deliver (acceptance evidence for a chosen experience), Tune (whole-experience tuning of a settled design through data-driven dials), and Review.
- **Settled premises:** a design assembled from mechanics proven in shipped games is tuned, not re-proved. Systems are built to verified with stand-ins; feel is judged in the whole encounter, level, or loop.
- **Independent gates:** the context that built a slice does not grade it. A fresh evaluator records a verdict and sealed predictions before the developer plays; feedback is compared against those predictions to calibrate future reads.
- **Engineering contracts:** capability boundaries (responsibility, mutation, lifetime, execution phase, failure behavior) and verification records bound to the revision actually tested. Stale evidence is stale, not passing.
- **Harness evaluation:** small regression tasks drawn from real failure classes, with valid controls and planted failures, and an explicit list of which checks are planned, advisory, or enforced.

# Provenance

This package adapts [Agentic Flow](https://theronjames.substack.com/p/agentic-flow), Theron James's spec-driven operating model for agent-assisted development, to AI-assisted indie game development.

Its main upstream source is the Outcome Spec template (v0.2) and companion templates in [theronjames-dev/agentic-sdlc-draft](https://github.com/theronjames-dev/agentic-sdlc-draft), published under the MIT license. Concepts carried over include separating deliverables from the results they produce for someone, separating learning from delivery, capability contracts at boundaries, and connecting evidence to decisions.

This package supplies newly written game-development guidance, formats, and hypothetical examples. It changes the process for experiential judgment, solo ownership, lightweight iteration, and AI implementation handoffs.

Research that informed the harness-evaluation guidance is cited in [references/harness-evaluation.md](references/harness-evaluation.md).
